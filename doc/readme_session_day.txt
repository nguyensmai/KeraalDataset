Chronological order of patient recordings
The dataset for group 1 has been collected from patients during a 3-weeks long rehabilitation program, with daily sessions of half-an-hour that are recorded for this dataset. Therefore, patients are recorded 15 days over 3 weeks.
Therefore, we can have a long-term analysis of patients performance with the chronological order of patient recordings.
This file describes which day each recording was done: days 0,1,2, 3 correspond to the beginning of their rehabilitation program, and days 9,10, 11, 12 correspond to the end of the 3-weeks program.

As a reminder, the naming of files for group1a is groupID/Modality/GroupId-Modality-ExerciseName-SubjectId-RecordingId.extension.
